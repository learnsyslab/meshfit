## Prior work meshfit builds on

- **RoMa** — Edstedt et al., CVPR 2024. The dense matcher meshfit uses to put
  a render in correspondence with a photograph.
- **BOP** — Hodaň et al. The benchmark convention of scoring symmetric objects
  over their equivalent poses, which is why meshfit reports yaw ambiguity
  instead of committing to one peak.
